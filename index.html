Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 25, 2026

Welcome to NEXORA. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and shared when you use the NEXORA Android application.

By using NEXORA, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy. Please read it carefully.

1. Information We Collect

NEXORA may process certain information to provide its features and improve the application experience.

  • Profile Information: The username and date of birth you enter during local profile setup may be stored on your device.
  • Device Information: Advertising and third-party SDKs may process device identifiers, technical information, and app interaction data according to their own policies and settings.
  • Voice Search: If you use microphone-based search, the app accesses your microphone after permission is granted. Speech recognition may process audio through your device's speech recognition service.
  • Search Information: Search terms and content identifiers may be processed to retrieve entertainment metadata.

2. Local Data Storage

Certain profile preferences and application settings may be stored locally on your device using Android storage technologies.

NEXORA does not require users to create an account or sign in with Google to use its core features.

Uninstalling the application may remove locally stored application data.

3. Microphone Permission

NEXORA may request microphone permission to provide voice search functionality.

  • Microphone access is requested when the relevant feature is used.
  • You can deny or revoke microphone permission through Android settings.
  • Voice recognition may be handled by an external speech recognition service.
  • Audio processing and retention are subject to the speech recognition provider's privacy practices.

4. Advertising Services

NEXORA may use Unity Ads to display advertisements. Depending on the SDK configuration, advertising services may process information such as device identifiers, advertising interactions, approximate location, and technical device information.

These services may use information to deliver, measure, and improve advertisements, subject to their own privacy policies and applicable consent requirements.

For more information, visit:
Unity Privacy Policy

5. Rewarded Ads and Ad-Free Access

NEXORA may offer an optional rewarded advertisement. After successful completion and confirmation of the advertising reward, users may receive 24 hours of advertising-free access to NEXORA-controlled advertising.

The access entitlement and its expiry information may be stored locally on your device.

Ad-free access applies to advertisements controlled by NEXORA. It does not guarantee removal of advertisements or promotional content served independently by third-party embedded video providers.

6. Third-Party Services and Video Providers

NEXORA may use third-party services to retrieve entertainment metadata and provide embedded playback.

  • TMDB: Used for movie, television, and entertainment metadata.
  • Third-Party Video Providers: Playback may be delivered through externally hosted embedded players.
  • Unity Ads: Used for advertising and rewarded access features.

When you interact with third-party services or embedded players, those providers may independently collect information under their own privacy policies.

NEXORA does not control the privacy practices, content, advertisements, or data handling of independent third-party providers.

TMDB information is subject to the TMDB Privacy Policy .

7. Data Sharing

NEXORA does not sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed or shared with relevant third-party service providers when necessary to operate application features, deliver advertisements, retrieve metadata, or provide embedded playback.

8. Data Security

We aim to use reasonable technical measures to protect information processed by the application. However, no electronic storage or internet transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data Retention

Locally stored profile information and preferences remain on your device until they are changed, cleared, or removed through application or device settings.

Information processed by third-party providers is retained according to their respective policies.

10. Children's Privacy

NEXORA is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through a registration system.

If you believe a child has provided personal information through a feature or service associated with NEXORA, please contact us.

11. Your Choices and Permissions

You can control certain application permissions through your Android device settings, including microphone permission.

You may also clear locally stored application data through Android settings or uninstall the application. Third-party advertising and service preferences may need to be managed separately with those providers.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when application features, services, or applicable requirements change. Any revised version will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
